Prostadine side effects and precautions

By [AUTHOR NAME, credentials] · Medically reviewed by [REVIEWER NAME, credentials], [date] · Updated [date]

Short answer: We found no published safety study of the finished product [VERIFY], so its side-effect profile can't be stated with confidence. The risks below are inferred from the ingredients: excess iodine affecting the thyroid, stomach upset, possible medication interactions, and delayed evaluation of symptoms that need a diagnosis.

Possible side effects, by source

Iodine and seaweed. Excess iodine can cause or worsen thyroid problems. Symptoms include fatigue, weight change, palpitations, heat or cold intolerance and neck swelling. Risk is higher with thyroid disease or long-term use.

Saw palmetto. Reported effects are generally mild: nausea, stomach discomfort, headache (NCCIH).

Contaminants. Seaweeds can take up arsenic and other metals. Ask whether third-party test results are available [VERIFY].

Shilajit and neem. Limited human safety data.

Allergy. Check the label if you are sensitive to seaweed, shellfish or iodine, and review the inactive ingredients.

Talk to a doctor or pharmacist first if you

  • have thyroid disease or take thyroid medicine, or take amiodarone (it contains iodine);
  • take blood thinners such as warfarin;
  • have surgery planned;
  • take prescription prostate medicine such as tamsulosin or finasteride (don't stop it without advice);
  • have kidney disease;
  • take several supplements that may also contain iodine.

Symptoms a supplement should not be used to manage

Urinary symptoms have many causes, including benign enlargement, infection, diabetes, medication effects and, less often, cancer. See a clinician promptly for blood in the urine, painful urination, fever with urinary symptoms, new back or pelvic pain, unexplained weight loss, or a worsening weak stream. Go to urgent care if you cannot urinate. A supplement should never delay a conversation about PSA testing or an exam.

If you have a reaction

Stop taking it and contact a healthcare professional. Report serious reactions to the FDA (MedWatch / Safety Reporting Portal) [VERIFY link].

What we don't know

We found no manufacturing audits, third-party purity testing, long-term safety data or interaction studies for the finished product [VERIFY]. Treat "quality-focused manufacturing" as unverified until a certificate of analysis or third-party seal is shown. See the ingredient evidence review and FAQ.
