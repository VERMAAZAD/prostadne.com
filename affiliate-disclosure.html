1. What Is an Affiliate Relationship?

An affiliate relationship is a marketing arrangement in which a website earns a commission or referral fee for directing visitors to a product or service. When you click a link on this website and subsequently make a purchase of Prostadine, this site may receive compensation.

Affiliate marketing is a standard, legal practice widely used across the internet. Importantly, the price you pay for Prostadine is never affected by whether or not we receive a commission — you pay the same price regardless.

2. Our Affiliate Relationships

3. FTC Compliance

This disclosure is provided in compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials in advertising, as set out in 16 CFR Part 255.

The FTC requires that websites disclose any material connection between a reviewer or endorser and the product being promoted. A "material connection" includes financial compensation, free products, or other benefits. We are committed to complying with these regulations and ensuring that our readers are fully informed.

FTC Disclosure Summary: This website has a material connection to Prostadine in the form of affiliate compensation for qualifying purchases made through links on this site.

4. Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships do not influence the content, reviews, or opinions presented on this website. We maintain editorial independence and strive to provide honest, accurate, and balanced information about Prostadine and prostate health topics in general.

Key principles we follow:

5. How We Earn Compensation

The exact commission structure varies by partnership agreement. We do not disclose specific commission rates or financial arrangements.
